Kitchen tips

Small details change the whole pairing.

Open a tip to see the practical reason behind it.

Very cold temperatures mute aroma. A slightly warmer pour lets citrus, herbs, flowers, and mineral notes become easier to notice.

The sauce often contributes more fat, acid, spice, and sweetness than the protein, so it should guide the final beverage choice.

Sparkling drinks can cut through richness and create a fresh transition between bites.

Acidity is one of the most flexible balancing tools for food because it refreshes the palate and keeps rich dishes from feeling heavy.
